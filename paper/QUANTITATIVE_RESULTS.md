## Method summary

1. **Phase 0** — outer-shell (0–300 km) log-mean σ_eff; skin depth δ=√(2/ωμσ); loss tangent tanδ=σ/(ωε); circumferential path attenuation; shell-path Q upper bound Q ≲ β/(2α) with β=ω√(με).

3. Profiles are literature-bracketed synthetics (optimistic / nominal / Apollo-style / pessimistic).

### Terminology note (for paper §3)

Across the nominal and Apollo-style envelopes at 1–30 Hz, **tanδ ≫ 1**: conduction current dominates. The outer shell is a **weakly conducting medium with large skin depth**, not a low-loss dielectric (which would require tanδ ≪ 1). Absolute attenuation lengths can still be hundreds of km because σ is small — but a lunar circumference is many skin depths for nominal profiles, so global shell-guided waves are heavily attenuated.

## Paper-ready claims

1. At 1–30 Hz, literature-bracketed outer-shell conductivities give **tanδ ≫ 1** (conduction-dominated): a **weak conductor with large skin depth**, not a low-loss dielectric.
2. Without an ionosphere (**Model A**), there is **no closed global cavity**.
3. Even with a **hypothetical** 100 km ionosphere (**Model B**), cavity Q is set by Re(Z_g)+Re(Z_i) and remains modest for conductive profiles; continuous σ(r) provides distributed wall loss.
4. Circumferential shell paths span many skin depths for nominal σ (half-circumference attenuations of tens to hundreds of dB), so shell-guided global standing waves are not supported.
5. Scope is **1-D radial stratification** (layered impedance + path integrals). Lateral heterogeneity is future work.
